EYE LINE

One-tap video recorder with live streaming and Google Drive backup for Android.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

EYE LINE records videos on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Data the app uses

  • Camera and microphone – only to record videos and live streams that you start.
  • Videos – saved on your phone in Movies/EyeLine. They stay on your device unless you choose to share or upload them.
  • Google account (optional) – if you connect Google Drive, the app uses Google Sign-In to get your name and email (shown in Settings) and permission to create files in your Drive.
  • Settings – such as WhatsApp numbers, stream keys and video title are stored only on your phone.

Google Drive

The app requests only the drive.file permission. It can see and manage only the files it uploads into the "EYE LINE" folder of your own Drive. It cannot read any other file in your Drive. Each user's videos go to that user's own Drive.

EYE LINE's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not run any server. Your videos and data are not sent to the developer.
  • We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.
  • Videos leave your phone only when you upload them to your Google Drive, share them (for example to WhatsApp), or live stream to a platform you configured.

Your control

You can delete videos in the app, sign out of Google Drive in Settings, and remove the app's Drive access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app removes its settings from your phone.
